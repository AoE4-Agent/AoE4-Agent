# Age of Empires IV chat taunts

Chat taunts are numbered lines the game speaks and translates. In chat, type a slash and the number, then send. Official support's rule is to type the slash: `/63` is "I'm under attack!". The example config uses that pair, and it matches the list below.

Some numbers are locked until a ranked season or a limited event. The in-game Taunt collection (Player Profile, or the pause menu in multiplayer) is the list the client will actually send. Typing a number previews the line before you send it. Taunts can be muted, and they have their own volume slider.

This is not a cheat list. Cheats are different chat strings, and they are off in Quick Match and Ranked. The support article linked below is the official cheat table.

Checked 14 Nov 2026. A line is in the table only when at least one source prints that number with that text. Gaps are called out instead of filled in.

| 115–119 events | Wiki names Summer Vacation, Map Monster, Hallow's Hearth, and Tons o' Toys. | Those event names were not repeated by the Steam comment that otherwise agrees on the words. |
